INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------

The stolen-key scenario intentionally passes the simulated higher layer because the attacker is modelled as possessing a valid target credential.

RF therefore supplies an independent RF-derived physical-layer consistency signal in that scenario. This is not proof of a hardware-unique fingerprint because the measured RF features are affected by channel and received-signal conditions.

A FLAG is an escalation signal, not a verified attack detection and not an autonomous rejection.
